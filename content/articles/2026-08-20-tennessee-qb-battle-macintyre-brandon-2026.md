## The Job Nobody Has Won Yet

Tennessee opens at home against Furman on September 5th, ranked 20th in the country, favored by 46.5 points. That spread tells you everything about the gap between these two programs.

It tells you nothing about who lines up under center for the Vols.

George MacIntyre and Faizon Brandon are still fighting for the starting job. Thirty days from kickoff. That is not a comfortable place for a program with SEC title ambitions.

## What Heupel Is Really Doing

Josh Heupel has never been shy about playing multiple quarterbacks when it suits him. But there is a difference between a coach managing a rotation and a coach who genuinely hasn't made up his mind.

Benjamin Watson over at SEC Network framed this as a development story. Heupel "continuing to develop" the candidates. That's the polite version.

Here's the impolite version: if you're ranked 20th and your quarterback competition isn't settled before fall camp ends, somebody in that room hasn't separated himself the way a program expects.

## MacIntyre vs. Brandon — What's Actually at Stake

MacIntyre brings familiarity with the system. He's operated inside Heupel's RPO framework and understands the tempo. The question with MacIntyre has always been whether he can make the throw when the defense takes away the easy answer.

Brandon is the athletic wildcard. Bigger upside. Bigger risk. The kind of quarterback who makes coordinators nervous for different reasons depending on which sideline they're standing on.

Neyland Stadium against Furman is not a stress test. The Paladins are a 46.5-point underdog. Nobody learns anything real from a game like that.

The uncomfortable question nobody in Knoxville wants to ask out loud: what happens in Week 3 when the schedule gets serious and the quarterback still has question marks attached to his name?

## The Spread Doesn't Lie About One Thing

Tennessee minus-46.5 against Furman, with a 66.5-point total. Vegas expects points. A lot of them. Heupel's offense produces regardless of who's handing off, but a quarterback who isn't trusted changes the ceiling of this thing.

Georgia opens that same weekend against Tennessee State, sitting at number three in the country. The Bulldogs have answers at the positions that matter.

The Vols need the same clarity.

Somebody has to win this job. The clock is running.
