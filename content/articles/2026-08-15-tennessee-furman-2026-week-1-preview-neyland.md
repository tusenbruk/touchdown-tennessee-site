## The Number

Neyland Stadium has a capacity of 102,455. The spread for the September 5th opener is 46.5. Do the math however you want — that's roughly one point for every 2,200 seats. The Paladins from Greenville, South Carolina are coming to Knoxville to get paid and go home.

That's the transaction. Nobody's pretending otherwise.

## What a 46.5-Point Spread Actually Means

Furman plays in the Southern Conference. They run the triple option. Their head coach has never faced a crowd bigger than 25,000. They are not a measuring stick for anything except whether Tennessee's backups can stay disciplined in the fourth quarter when it's 49-7 and 87,000 people are already in the parking lot.

The over/under sits at 66.5. That's the books telling you Tennessee is expected to score somewhere north of 55 points. On September 5th. In game one. Against a team that's a 10-to-1 moneyline underdog.

For context: Furman's entire 2025 season scoring output was 312 points. In 11 games.

## The Actual Story Here

The Vols open on SECN+. Not ESPN. Not ABC. Not even the main SEC Network. The streaming overflow channel that your uncle can never figure out how to find on his television.

That is a program statement. Not a good one. Tennessee has been relevant enough in recent years to warrant better than streaming-only for a home opener. This placement means the SEC looked at this game and put it in the same drawer as September scrimmages nobody asked for.

Down in Athens, Georgia is playing Tennessee State — also on SECN+, also a 45.5-point spread — which means the conference scheduled back-to-back Tennessee teams as cupcake fodder on the same Saturday afternoon. Somebody in Birmingham thought that was fine.

## What to Actually Watch For

Neyland in September is its own thing. The heat comes off that artificial turf like a griddle. The student section fills in loud and then empties out when it gets boring, which against Furman will happen around the 9-minute mark of the second quarter.

The question worth answering on September 5th isn't whether Tennessee wins. It's who looks like they earned a starting role by the time the fourth quarter rolls around. Depth charts get clarified in these games. Pecking orders get established.

Furman will show up, cash the guarantee check, and drive back down I-40. Tennessee will win by somewhere between 40 and 60 points.

The real season starts the week after.
