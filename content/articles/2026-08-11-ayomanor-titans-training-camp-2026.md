## He Came Back Different

Stan Ayomanor showed up to Saint Thomas Sports Park looking like a man with something to prove. Good. That's the right energy.

His rookie season was exactly what you'd expect from a young receiver still learning the speed of the NFL game. Flashes. Disappearing acts. Too many plays where he was almost there. Almost doesn't count in this league.

But camp tells you things the stat sheet can't. And right now, Ayomanor is the name people keep saying.

## What Changed

The offseason happened. A laser-focused one, by all accounts.

That phrase gets thrown around every August. Every guy who struggled says he put in the work. Most of them are telling the truth. The difference is whether the work was the right work.

With Ayomanor, you can see it. Route running that's sharper. Hands that look more reliable. A body that moves like it belongs on this field instead of still auditioning for it.

He's not just running faster. He's thinking faster. That's the leap that matters.

## What It Means for the Offense

The Titans need a legitimate second option in the passing game. They need someone who can win individual battles when defenses decide to take away the primary read.

If Ayomanor is genuinely standing out above every other receiver on this roster right now, that's significant. Camp competition has a way of separating want-to from actually-can.

He's not beating backups either. He's beating corners who will be on rosters in September.

## The Caution Flag

Training camp is training camp. Pads come on and schemes get complicated and August heat has a way of exposing who was real and who was rehearsed.

We've seen receivers light up camp and then vanish once the regular season starts. We've also seen guys find themselves in camp and carry it all the way through January.

Ayomanor has the physical tools. He always did. The question was always between his ears. If the offseason addressed that — if he came back locked in rather than just hoping — then this Titans offense just got more interesting.

## The Next Question

Can he do this when the preseason lights come on and a real defensive coordinator is trying to take him away?

That answer comes soon. Watch his first few routes under pressure. Watch how he responds to the first bad drop.

Rookies reveal themselves in Year One. Year Two reveals who they actually are.
