## Atlanta, December 2001

Derek Dooley was still just a tight ends coach. Phillip Fulmer had gray in his hair but not much doubt. Tennessee went to the Peach Bowl against Michigan ranked eleventh in the country, which felt like a slight at the time.

Nobody talks about that now.

## What the Game Actually Was

The final score was 45-17. People remember the score. They don't remember that Michigan came in with a defense ranked in the top ten nationally and left the Georgia Dome looking like they'd been in a car wreck.

Casey Clausen threw for 270 yards. Kelley Washington caught everything in sight. Travis Stephens ran for 111 yards and two touchdowns and looked like the kind of back that made you believe the program had solved something.

It hadn't solved anything. But it didn't know that yet.

## The Thing Nobody Says Out Loud

Tennessee went to fourteen straight bowl games between 1994 and 2007. Won some. Lost plenty. But the 2001 Peach Bowl sits at a particular hinge point that doesn't get examined closely enough.

After that game, the program won bowl games in 2004 and 2007. Technically. But the 2001 game was the last time Tennessee went into a bowl and looked categorically better than a legitimate opponent from a power conference. Not lucky. Not close. Better.

That distinction matters.

## What Fulmer Said

He talked about the seniors. He always talked about the seniors. There were twenty-two of them on that roster and they'd been through the 1998 title and the losing and the recruiting wars and they went out in Atlanta playing loose.

Loose is rare in bowl games. Bowl games make teams tight.

This one didn't.

## The Longer View

The drought people reference — the one that ran through Fulmer's decline and Kiffin's disaster and Dooley's tenure and Butch Jones's slow unraveling — it didn't start the day after this game. But when you trace backward from 2023, from Josh Heupel finally breaking the streak against Clemson, the 2001 Peach Bowl is the last clean reference point.

The last game where Tennessee just showed up and was the better football team without any asterisk.

That's not nothing. Most programs never get one of those.

The question worth sitting with is whether 2023 is the start of a new run of those games, or just another clean exit before something complicated.

We'll find out.
